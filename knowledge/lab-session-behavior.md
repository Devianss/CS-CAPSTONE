# Lab session behavior

## Session focus

During scheduled lab time, students should stay on task. Runa can help with course concepts, tooling, and file organization inside **Runa_Folder**—not with bypassing lab policy or blocklists.

## Asking for help

If Runa refuses a request or queues an action for approval, note the **approval ID** (if shown) and contact lab staff on the instructor machine. The audit log records proposed and executed actions for traceability.

## Data minimization

Chat and audit events are designed for **governance and safety**, not bulk surveillance. Event descriptions summarize what happened without unnecessary personal detail.
